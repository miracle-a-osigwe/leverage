# Leverage

The high-stakes communication, meeting prep, and contract review copilot for founders and career professionals under pressure.

## The Unique Kernel
Dual-axis real-time communication synthesis (Firmness × Diplomacy) with visual Subtext Hazard Heatmapping (flagging passive aggression, emotional hedging, and unforced concessions) coupled with a dynamic Leverage Index and tactical rationale.

## Who It's For
* **Early-Stage Founders:** Navigating high-stress investor communication, awkward board dynamics, and aggressive term sheets without panic or unforced concessions.
* **Ambitious Career Professionals / Employees:** Addressing workplace grievances, compensation renegotiations, and tricky employment covenants without damaging relationships or sounding defensive.

Today, people either vent impulsively in draft emails (risking relationships and leverage) or rely on generic AI prompts that output bland, robotic platitudes.

## The Core Loop
1. User selects their persona (**Founder** or **Employee/Career**) and pastes an unfiltered, emotionally charged draft or clicks a 1-click realistic scenario preset.
2. The app renders an instant **Subtext Radiograph**, highlighting hazardous phrases with categorized risk badges (`[Passive Aggression]`, `[Defensive Hedging]`, `[Unforced Concession]`).
3. User dials dual interactive sliders: **Firmness** (0–100%) and **Diplomacy** (0–100%).
4. The message dynamically transforms into high-leverage executive prose, accompanied by an updated **Leverage Index** (e.g. 24% → 94%) and a clear *Tactical Rationale* explaining why the phrasing protects their position.
5. User copies the refined text with one click.

## Inspiration & Identity
* **Aesthetic:** Rare, tactile "Swiss Editorial / Executive Briefing" aesthetic. Warm alabaster and fine parchment base (`#fbfaf8` / `#f3efe6`), crisp architectural borders (`#e5dfd5`), deep carbon ink typography (`#191817`), and surgical editorial accent badges (crimson vermilion for hazards, warm amber-ochre for caution, and deep forest sage for high leverage). Stands completely apart from the sea of generic dark-mode SaaS dashboards.
* **Tone:** Calm, authoritative, discreet, and surgical. Feels like a confidential diplomatic cable or white-shoe executive counsel memo.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
To demonstrate engineering-grade execution and win 1st place in Devpost's "Build With AI: Basics" hackathon by delivering a memorable, tactile proof of concept that proves AI can guide human negotiation leverage through intuitive controls rather than generic chat bubbles.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a 60-to-90 second video, a viewer sees:
1. An aggressive, emotional email draft pasted into the editor.
2. Subtext hazard badges immediately lighting up with a low initial Leverage Index (28%).
3. Smooth slider adjustments (Firmness 85%, Diplomacy 90%) transforming the text into a polished, firm, collaborative message while the Leverage Index surges to 94%.
4. Fast 15-second tours through the preloaded **Objection Simulator** (stress-testing tough investor/manager questions) and **Clause Radar** (flagging gotchas in contracts), proving the cohesive product experience.

## The POC Boundary
* **IN:**
  * Clean, responsive single-page application with immediate reactive feedback.
  * Dual persona toggle (**Founder** vs. **Employee**).
  * Flagship Hero: **Subtext Equalizer** with phrase-level hazard highlights, dual sliders (**Firmness** / **Diplomacy**), real-time rewritten message, tactical rationale card, and dynamic **Leverage Index**.
  * Pillar 2: **Objection Simulator** with preloaded scenarios, adversary stress-tests, and winning counter-frames.
  * Pillar 3: **Clause Radar** with contract snippet scanner, Red/Amber/Green risk flags, and equitable counter-clause generator.
  * Realistic 1-click sample presets across all modes for frictionless demoing and testing.
  * One-click copy-to-clipboard and zero-login local state.

## Later
* Multi-document PDF / DOCX contract parser.
* Full conversation voice simulator with speech synthesis.
* Chrome / Gmail / Slack native extensions.
* User accounts and team negotiation playbooks.

## Explicitly Cut
* **User Authentication & Cloud Database:** Adds setup friction and auth edge cases with zero value to the 1-3 minute demo video.
* **Full File Upload / OCR:** Parsing formatting errors can break demos; focused clause pasting proves the core value cleanly.
* **Live OAuth Integrations:** Connecting live Gmail or Slack workspaces introduces external token dependencies and security friction; standalone web app is reliable and fast to evaluate.
