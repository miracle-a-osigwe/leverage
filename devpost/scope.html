Leverage

The high-stakes communication, meeting prep, and contract review copilot for founders and career professionals under pressure.
STATUS: DRAFT → READY FOR APPROVAL DOC: SCOPE.MD COMPANION THEME: SWISS EDITORIAL / ALABASTER

The Unique Kernel

Dual-axis real-time communication synthesis (Firmness × Diplomacy) with a visual Subtext Radiograph flagging emotional traps (Passive Aggression, Defensive Hedging, Unforced Concession) paired with an instant Leverage Index (24% → 94%) and strategic rationale.

Why this isn't generic: Rather than another passive chat box, the user gets tactile, calibrated control over tone and power dynamics with clear visual evidence of why the refined phrasing protects their leverage.

The Core Loop (Hero Journey)

STEP 01
Select & Paste
Pick persona (Founder/Employee) & paste raw unfiltered draft, or click a 1-click scenario preset.
STEP 02
Subtext Scan
Instant hazard heat-map flags emotional risks & displays baseline Leverage Index (e.g. 28%).
STEP 03
Dual-Axis Sliders
User dials Firmness (0–100%) and Diplomacy (0–100%) to calibrate their strategic stance.
STEP 04
Live Synthesis
Text re-synthesizes in real time. Leverage Index surges to 94% with an executive tactical rationale.

Target Personas

Early-Stage Founder

Navigating tense investor updates, delayed wire follow-ups, and aggressive term sheets without showing panic or conceding control.

Career Professional / Employee

Communicating workplace grievances, salary discussions, or unfair non-compete clauses firmly and diplomatically without risking career capital.

Scope Boundary

  • Single-Page Reactive Application: Swiss Editorial / Alabaster aesthetic with zero latency.
  • Persona Switcher: Founder & Employee modes.
  • Hero Pillar — Subtext Equalizer: Hazard badges, dual interactive sliders (Firmness × Diplomacy), live rewrite, tactical rationale, and dynamic Leverage Index.
  • Pillar 2 — Objection Simulator: Preloaded scenarios for investor burn-rate questions & salary/gap curveballs.
  • Pillar 3 — Clause Radar: Snippet scanner flagging red/amber risks with 1-click equitable counter-clauses.
  • 1-Click Presets & Copy: Zero friction for judges in the 90-second demo video.
  • Multi-page PDF contract uploads & OCR parser.
  • Interactive speech-to-speech audio simulation.
  • Native Gmail, Slack, and Outlook browser extensions.
  • Team negotiation history and analytics.
  • User Accounts & Cloud Auth: Creates login friction and credentials risk; unnecessary for demonstrating the working PoC.
  • Full Document Parsing: High error rate on weird PDF layouts; raw clause paste proves the kernel reliably.
  • Live OAuth Integrations: External API dependencies slow down demo review and testing.
